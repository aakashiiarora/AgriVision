1. Processing, Wireless Telemetry & Vision

Master Computing and Image Streaming Systems

PROCESSING CORE
MCU-01
Core Microcontroller

ESP32 DevKit v1 (30-Pin)

Master field robot controller. Handles telemetry polling, sensor aggregation, Wi-Fi SoftAP server, Blynk IoT cloud link, and motor H-bridge PWM.

CPU: Tensilica Xtensa Dual-Core 240MHz
Operating Voltage: 3.3V Logic (5V MicroUSB / Vin)
Connectivity: Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n & BLE 4.2
ADC Resolution: 12-bit (0 – 4095 range)
CAM-01
Optical Surveillance

ESP32-CAM (OV2640 Module)

Autonomous optical inspection camera. Streams real-time MJPEG video on local IP Port 81 for live crop health and furrow surveillance.

Camera Sensor: OmniVision OV2640 (2 Megapixel)
Image Array: 1600 × 1200 UXGA
Streaming Port: TCP Port 81 (/stream)
Flash Light: Onboard Bright White LED

2. Agricultural Environmental & Soil Sensors

Microclimate, Gas Analysis & Subsurface Moisture

SENSOR SUITE
SNS-01
Microclimate

DHT11 Temperature & Humidity Sensor

Measures ambient air temperature and relative humidity directly above the crop canopy.

Temp Range: 0°C to 50°C (±2°C accuracy)
Humidity Range: 20% to 90% RH (±5% accuracy)
Interface: Single-Bus Digital (ESP32 GPIO 4)
Starting Baseline: 26.5°C / 58%
SNS-02
Combustible Gas

MQ2 Combustible Gas & Smoke Sensor

Detects methane, LPG, and field fire smoke. Protects agricultural fields from brushfires and equipment fuel leaks.

Detection: LPG, Methane, Smoke, Alcohol
Sensitivity: 300 to 10000 ppm
Analog Pin: ESP32 GPIO 32 (ADC1_CH4)
Starting Baseline: 312 ADC
SNS-03
Air Quality

MQ135 Hazardous Air Quality Sensor

Monitors ammonia gas from fertilizer breakdown, nitrogen oxides, carbon dioxide, and air pollution in agricultural greenhouses and fields.

Gases: NH3, NOx, Alcohol, Benzene, Smoke, CO2
Concentration: 10 to 1000 ppm
Analog Pin: ESP32 GPIO 35 (ADC1_CH7)
Starting Baseline: 428 ADC
SNS-04
Live Hardware

Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor v1.2

Corrosion-resistant soil moisture probe extending down into root-zone soil. Measures volumetric moisture without metal electrolysis degradation.

Sensor Tech: Capacitive Dielectric Permittivity
Operating Voltage: 3.3V to 5.5V DC
Hardware Pin: ESP32 GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6)
IoT Link: Blynk Virtual Pin V1

3. Obstacle Avoidance, Locomotion & Power System

Sonar Spatial Sensing, 4WD Motor Actuation & Solar Battery

ROVER HARDWARE
ACT-01
Spatial Sonar

Dual HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensors (x2)

Mounted on Port and Starboard front bumpers. Measures obstacle proximity in centimeters to prevent furrow collisions.

Range: 2 cm – 400 cm (±3mm accuracy)
Sensor 1 (Port): Trig: GPIO 5 • Echo: GPIO 18 (74 cm)
Sensor 2 (Stbd): Trig: GPIO 19 • Echo: GPIO 21 (96 cm)
Frequency: 40 kHz Ultrasound Burst
ACT-02
Motor Driver

L298N Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver

Drives four geared DC locomotion motors with independent directional and PWM speed control from ESP32 GPIOs.

Driver Chip: L298N Dual H-Bridge IC
Motor Voltage: 7V – 12V DC
Peak Current: 2A per bridge (continuous 1.2A)
Controls: IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4, ENA, ENB
PWR-01
Power Subsystem

3S 18650 Li-ion Battery & LM2596 Buck

Rechargeable high-current field battery with Battery Management System (BMS) for overcurrent protection, stepped down to 5V via LM2596.

Nominal Voltage: 11.1V (12.6V Fully Charged)
Capacity: 2600 mAh (28.8 Wh)
Step-Down Buck: LM2596 DC-DC (Outputs 5.0V 3A)
Field Runtime: 3.5+ Hours Continuous Operation
PWR-02
Renewable Energy

6V 2W Monocrystalline Solar Panel

Roof-mounted photovoltaic panel providing auxiliary trickle charging under outdoor sunlight during crop surveillance missions.

Peak Voltage: 6.0V DC
Peak Current: 330 mA
Cell Efficiency: 19.5% Monocrystalline Silicon
Protection: 1N5819 Schottky Reverse Diode
